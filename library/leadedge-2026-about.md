**One-line:** A growth equity firm's eight-point screen for what it will look at: real scale, 25%+ growth at breakeven, 70%+ gross margin, recurring revenue with 90%+ gross retention, a diversified customer base and capital efficiency.

**Why it matters to my process/portfolio:** The first growth-equity voice in the vault, and the concrete case behind the owner's ask that growth-stage lessons surface for growth-stage companies and stay out of mature or early-stage ones. It turns "is this a quality growth business" from a narrative into a checkable screen.

## Takeaways

- **Lead Edge Capital calls itself a growth equity firm with $9 billion of assets under management, investing in software, internet and tech-enabled businesses.** (homepage, section hero) [verified]
- **It invests $50 million to $400 million per company, in both private and public technology companies.** Listing status does not define the mandate; the company's operating profile does. (section How We Invest) [verified]
- **The firm seeks companies meeting "most of" eight criteria:** revenue $10M+; 25%+ YoY growth; 70%+ gross margins; breakeven or profitable; SaaS or highly recurring revenue; 90%+ gross retention; a diversified customer base; capital efficiency, meaning scale relative to cash burn. (section The Lead Edge 8) [verified]
  - *Texture:* the bar is growth reached without burning cash. 25% growth at breakeven is a looser test than the Rule of 40 (it scores 25), so the screen rewards efficiency over raw speed.
- **The page does not say how many of the eight count as "most".** (section The Lead Edge 8) [verified]
  - *Texture:* secondary profiles say five or six; not on the firm's own pages, so not carried as fact.
